Swimming Pool

Our rooftop swimming pool at Hotel Beach Way is one of Cox's Bazar's finest leisure facilities. Offering breathtaking panoramic views of the Bay of Bengal, it is the perfect retreat for guests of all ages — from families with young children to couples seeking a peaceful escape by the water. The pool maintains hygienic standards year-round and is staffed by trained attendants to ensure safety and comfort at all times.

Whether you prefer a vigorous morning swim or a leisurely afternoon float, our pool is open daily from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Comfortable sunbeds, fresh towels, and light refreshments from our poolside service are available to complete your experience.

We Give The Best Services

At Hotel Beach Way, every service is crafted with attention to detail and genuine hospitality. From the moment you step into our lobby to the time you relax poolside, our team is dedicated to making your stay exceptional in every way.

Our poolside area includes a dedicated children's wading zone, lounge seating with umbrellas, and direct access to our Dew Drop Restaurant for in-pool dining. Guests can request pool toys, floats, and safety equipment from our front desk at no additional charge.

We understand that a great holiday means having everything you need within reach. That is why our pool service is complemented by our 24/7 room service, complimentary Wi-Fi, and regular housekeeping — ensuring a seamless and luxurious stay throughout your visit to Cox's Bazar.
