OUR JOURNEY

A Decade of Warmth,
Hospitality & Excellence

From a humble dream to Cox's Bazar's most beloved coastal retreat — explore the milestones that shaped Hotel Beach Way into the award-winning destination it is today.

2013
Foundation

Grand Opening — Hotel Beach Way Is Born

With a vision to redefine coastal hospitality, Hotel Beach Way opened its doors on Kolatoli Road, Cox's Bazar in 2013. Built to blend modern comfort with the natural beauty of Bangladesh's longest sea beach, the hotel welcomed its first guests with 30 premium rooms, a 24-hour front desk, and a commitment to world-class service.

30 Rooms Launched 24/7 Front Desk Kolatoli Beach Access
Dining

Dew Drop Restaurant Opens Its Doors

In 2015, Hotel Beach Way unveiled the Dew Drop Restaurant — a full-service dining experience offering a rich spread of local Bangladeshi flavors and international cuisine. The restaurant quickly became a landmark on Kolatoli Road, attracting both hotel guests and city locals. A complimentary buffet breakfast service was also introduced for all room packages.

Dew Drop Restaurant Buffet Breakfast BBQ Evenings
2015
2017
Expansion

Premium Room Renovation & Suite Expansion

2017 marked a major transformation. All existing rooms were upgraded with premium furnishings, smart TV systems, improved air conditioning, and sea-inspired interior décor. A new wing was added, introducing the Honeymoon Suite and the Executive Suite — elevating Hotel Beach Way to a full boutique luxury experience for couples and business travelers alike.

Honeymoon Suite Added Executive Suite Smart Room Upgrades
Recreation

Swimming Pool & Recreation Centre Inaugurated

Responding to growing guest demand, 2019 saw the launch of our outdoor swimming pool and dedicated jogging track — completing Hotel Beach Way's transformation into a true resort-style destination. Guests could now enjoy morning laps, poolside relaxation, and beachside walks all within the hotel premises, making every stay an enriching wellness experience.

Outdoor Pool Jogging Track Resort Experience
2019
2021
Recognition

Award-Winning Hospitality & Guest Excellence

By 2021, Hotel Beach Way had earned the trust of thousands of guests from across Bangladesh and beyond. The hotel received formal recognition as one of Cox's Bazar's highest-rated hospitality destinations, celebrated for its exceptional service quality, clean and comfortable accommodations, and consistent 5-star guest reviews on travel platforms.

Best Hotel Award Top-Rated on Travel Platforms Guest Excellence
Wellness & Events

Spa, Gym & Conference Hall Launched

2023 brought our most ambitious expansion yet. Hotel Beach Way opened a state-of-the-art Spa & Massage Centre, a fully equipped modern Gym, and a multipurpose Conference & Banquet Hall. These additions positioned the hotel as a premier destination not only for leisure travelers but also for corporate retreats, business events, and social celebrations.

Spa & Massage Gym Centre Conference Hall
2023
2025
A Decade & Beyond

Celebrating Excellence — The Journey Continues

Over a decade after opening our doors, Hotel Beach Way stands as a symbol of warmth, quality, and coastal luxury in Cox's Bazar. With a dedicated team, continuously upgraded facilities, and thousands of happy memories made by guests from around the world, we look forward to the next chapter — growing stronger, serving better, and welcoming every guest like family.

10+ Years of Service 10,000+ Happy Guests Continuously Growing
