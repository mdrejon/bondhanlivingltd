Welcome to the Deluxe Double Room at Hotel Beach Way — your perfect coastal retreat in Cox's Bazar. This elegantly appointed room features a plush King-size bed with headboard, premium bedding, and thoughtfully designed interiors that blend modern comfort with coastal warmth. Ideal for couples and solo travelers seeking a luxurious yet affordable stay.

Each Deluxe Double Room is equipped with a comfortable sofa, tea table, wall cabinet, and a spacious bathroom with premium fixtures. Stay connected with complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi, enjoy your favourite shows on Cable LED TV, and keep refreshments chilled in the Mini Fridge with Mini Bar. The powerful A/C and ceiling fan ensure your comfort regardless of the season.

Located just 5 minutes from Kolatoli Beach and 10 minutes from Cox's Bazar Airport, Hotel Beach Way offers the perfect base for exploring the world's longest unbroken sandy beach while enjoying all the comforts of a premium boutique hotel.
